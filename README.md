# Simple Interest Calculator

A straightforward calculator that computes simple interest based on principal amount, annual interest rate, and time period.

## Formula

```
Simple Interest = (P × T × R) / 100
```

Where:
- **P** = Principal amount (initial investment)
- **T** = Time period (in years)
- **R** = Annual rate of interest (as a percentage)

## Usage

Provide the following inputs:
- **Principal (p)**: The initial amount of money
- **Time (t)**: Duration in years
- **Rate (r)**: Annual interest rate (%)

The calculator will output the simple interest earned.

### Example
- Principal: $1,000
- Time: 2 years
- Rate: 5% per annum
- **Result**: Simple Interest = $100

# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We as members, contributors, and leaders are committed to making this simple interest calculator project a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free community. We value respectful collaboration and believe that diverse perspectives strengthen our work.

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

* Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
* Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
* Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience
* Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community
* Writing clear and professional comments in issues and pull requests
* Providing helpful code reviews that are constructive and encouraging

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
* Public or private harassment
* Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting
* Spam or excessive self-promotion
* Deliberate disruption of discussions or code reviews

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, including:

* GitHub Issues and Pull Requests
* Project Discussions
* Comments on commits and code reviews
* Any communication channels related to this project

This Code of Conduct also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces (e.g., using an official project email, posting via official social media accounts, or acting as an appointed representative at online or offline events).

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. They will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, pull requests, and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct. Community leaders will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## How to Report

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the community leader responsible for enforcement:

**Email**: tendaisg@gmail.com

When reporting, please include:
* A description of the incident
* The username(s) involved
* Any relevant links (issue, PR, comment)
* The context and impact of the behavior
* Any additional details that would help with the investigation

**Confidentiality**: All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Community leaders are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter and will handle all reports confidentially.

## Investigation Timeline

* **24-48 hours**: Initial acknowledgment of your report
* **5-7 business days**: Investigation and preliminary findings
* **7-14 business days**: Resolution and follow-up communication with parties involved

## Enforcement Guidelines

Community leaders will follow these Community Impact Guidelines in determining the consequences for any action they deem in violation of this Code of Conduct. **Enforcement is critical to maintaining a healthy community**, and all violations will be addressed consistently and fairly.

### 1. Correction (First Offense)

**Community Impact**: Use of inappropriate language or other behavior deemed unprofessional or unwelcome in the community.

**Investigation**: The incident will be reviewed for context and severity.

**Consequence**: A private, written warning from community leaders via email, providing clarity around the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested. The violator will be given 48 hours to respond.

**Follow-up**: Community leaders will monitor the account for 30 days. Repeated violations at this level will result in escalation.

### 2. Warning (Repeated or Moderate Violations)

**Community Impact**: A violation through a single serious incident or series of inappropriate actions, or repeated violations despite previous warnings.

**Investigation**: All previous interactions and incidents will be reviewed to determine pattern of behavior.

**Consequence**: A formal warning with clear consequences for continued behavior. The individual will have:
- No interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction
- Mandatory communication pause (typically 7-14 days)
- Restriction from engaging in community spaces as defined by leaders

Violating these terms will result in temporary or permanent ban.

**Follow-up**: Community leaders will monitor for 60 days. Any further violations will trigger immediate escalation.

### 3. Temporary Ban (Serious or Sustained Violations)

**Community Impact**: A serious violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior, harassment of an individual, or multiple violations within a short timeframe.

**Investigation**: A comprehensive review of all conduct will be conducted, including previous warnings and patterns of behavior.

**Consequence**: A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public communication with the community for a specified period (typically 1-3 months). The individual:
- Cannot participate in issues, pull requests, or discussions
- Cannot access community communication channels
- Must acknowledge the violation and agree to reformed behavior before reinstatement

**Reinstatement**: After the ban period, the individual may request reinstatement by demonstrating genuine commitment to the Code of Conduct. Reinstatement is not guaranteed.

**Follow-up**: Upon reinstatement, all interactions will be closely monitored for 90 days.

### 4. Permanent Ban (Egregious or Repeated Serious Violations)

**Community Impact**: Demonstrating a pattern of violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior, harassment of an individual, aggression toward classes of individuals, or violations that cause significant harm to the community.

**Investigation**: A thorough investigation will be conducted, including all evidence and context.

**Consequence**: A permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the community. The individual's contributions may be removed at the discretion of community leaders.

**Appeal Process**: Permanent bans can be appealed after one year, but reinstatement is exceptional and requires demonstrated, sustained behavioral change outside this community.

## Enforcement Philosophy

We believe in:
- **Consistency**: Similar violations receive similar consequences
- **Proportionality**: Consequences match the severity of the violation
- **Transparency**: Decisions are explained to all parties
- **Redemption**: We offer opportunities for people to learn and improve
- **Community Safety**: When necessary, we prioritize community safety over individual participation

## Appeals

If you believe a moderation decision was made in error, you may appeal within 7 days by emailing tendaisg@gmail.com with:
* The original decision being appealed
* Your reasoning for the appeal
* Any new information relevant to the case

An appeal will be reviewed by a different community leader if possible.
